# Earth Reference Case

## Why Earth belongs in this repository

Earth is the one planetary case for which life is independently established.

Therefore Earth provides a calibration target for the examination framework.

## Atmospheric reference

The modern atmosphere contains approximately:

- N2 as the dominant gas
- O2 at about 21% by volume
- trace CO2
- trace CH4
- H2O with strongly variable abundance
- trace N2O and other reactive species

## Why the combination matters

The important property is not any one gas.

It is the coexistence of chemically reactive species maintained far from equilibrium by continuous fluxes through the biosphere, atmosphere, ocean and surface.

## Teaching questions

1. What gases are present?
2. Which reactions would consume them?
3. How rapidly would those reactions proceed?
4. What replenishes the gases?
5. Which sources are biological?
6. Which processes could be abiotic?
7. Can the complete Earth atmospheric state be reproduced abiotically?
8. Which observations most strongly constrain the alternatives?

## Exoplanet comparison

An exoplanet should be compared to Earth molecule-by-molecule and system-by-system.

Do not ask:

"Does it look like Earth?"

Ask:

"Which measured properties agree with Earth, which differ, and which competing abiotic models survive?"
