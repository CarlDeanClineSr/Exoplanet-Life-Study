# Atmospheric Chemistry Examination

## The Earth-like disequilibrium panel

A high-value panel for terrestrial planets is:

**H2O + CO2 + N2 + O2/O3 + CH4 + N2O + CO + NOx/HNO3**

The purpose is not to claim that this combination proves life.

The purpose is to determine whether the complete atmospheric state is chemically self-consistent under known abiotic physics.

## Key relationships

### Oxygen and methane

O2 and CH4 are continuously consumed by oxidation chemistry.

A persistent combination of substantial O2 and CH4 therefore requires continuous replenishment.

### Oxygen and ozone

O3 is closely related to O2 photochemistry and can provide an additional constraint on the oxygen system.

### Carbon monoxide

CO is an important abiotic diagnostic.

CO2 photolysis can produce CO and oxygen-bearing products. A substantial CO abundance can therefore affect interpretation of apparent oxygen biosignatures.

### Nitrogen chemistry

Track the chain:

N2 -> NO -> NO2 -> HNO3

and related nitrogen chemistry including N2O and NH3.

HNO3 and NO2 can be scientifically useful atmospheric constraints, but they are not biological proof by themselves.

### Nitrous oxide

N2O is an important candidate biosignature gas because biological nitrogen cycling produces much of Earth's atmospheric N2O.

Abiotic production pathways must nevertheless be tested.

## Required data fields per molecule

- Molecular species
- Detection status
- Best-fit abundance
- Lower/upper uncertainty
- Wavelength region
- Spectral resolution
- Instrument
- Observation epoch
- Atmospheric model
- Photochemical lifetime
- Known sources
- Known sinks
- Abiotic pathways
- Confidence notes

## Do not collapse uncertainty

A weak spectral feature should remain weak.

Do not turn:

"possible CH4"

into:

"CH4 detected"

and do not turn:

"CH4 + O2"

into:

"life."

## Chemical disequilibrium

For mature systems, calculate or estimate:

- equilibrium composition
- observed composition
- redox imbalance
- atmospheric chemical lifetimes
- available Gibbs free energy, where the model supports it

All assumptions must be recorded.
